Utilization of renewable natural gas, or RNG, in the United States has been growing at an increasing rate since 2022. While this growth could be attributed to many factors, the federal incentives provided by the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) in 2022 seem to have provided the optimal environment for RNG to grow rapidly.
