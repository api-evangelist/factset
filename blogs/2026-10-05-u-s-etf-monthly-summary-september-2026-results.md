U.S.-listed ETFs closed September with $16.4 trillion in total assets under management, which is relatively flat compared to August month end . Monthly net fund flows continued their decline with a significant drop in September: just $140.9 billion in new assets added. This is down 29.6% from $182.6 billion in new assets in August.
